# Campaign design

The first release of the revised rules is intentionally small: five levels, one or two blocks per board, and one matching goal per block.

The difficulty curve is solver-verified:

| Level | Name | Minimum moves | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | HOME | 1 | Learn the swipe-to-gravity input with one block. |
| 2 | TURN | 3 | Introduce two independently matched colours. |
| 3 | BRAKE | 4 | Show that contact is a brake, not a win. |
| 4 | CROSS | 6 | Plan the order of two routes. |
| 5 | TWO PATHS | 8 | Coordinate both blocks through shared gravity. |

The engine rejects boards with more than two blocks, duplicate block colours, missing goals, extra goals, wildcard pieces, and removed win modes.
